I acknowledge the use of generative AI tools in completing this assessment.
Details of which tools were used and how they were used are provided in the
table below and the accompanying description of their roles. I take
responsibility for critically evaluating and integrating the AI-generated
content, and ensuring it adheres to academic integrity standards.

\subsection*{Use of AI in this thesis}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Scope:} This declaration covers the preparation of this thesis,
including the proposal material developed earlier and the subsequent report
revision process.
\item \textbf{Research question refinement:} ChatGPT-4o and Gemini were used
to test alternative phrasing and improve precision of the research question.
\item \textbf{Literature review support:} NotebookLM, Gemini and ChatGPT-4o
were used to summarise and compare relevant papers during preparation of the
research background and literature review.
\item \textbf{Language editing:} ChatGPT-4o, Gemini and Cursor AI were used
to improve grammar, style and clarity of academic writing, as recorded in the
project proposal.
\item \textbf{Content creation and formatting:} Cursor AI assisted with
\LaTeX{} code editing, structural section updates and formatting tasks,
including adjustments to tables and page layout. Gemini and ChatGPT-4o were
also used to draft and revise text snippets during preparation of the proposal.
\item \textbf{Report revision:} Codex assisted with substantial chapter
rewrites, \LaTeX{} editing, reference verification and consistency checks
against saved experimental outputs during revision of the thesis. QuillBot
was also used to produce a paraphrased example for comparison during the
writing revision process.
\item \textbf{Accountability:} All final decisions, technical judgements and
submitted content remain the author's responsibility, including the assessment
of AI suggestions against the experimental records and cited sources.
\end{itemize}
