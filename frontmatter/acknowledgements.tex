I thank my supervisor, Professor Gianluca Demartini, for his guidance on the
design and development of this research project. His recommendations to
extend the evaluation to smaller models and include real face images helped
broaden the experimental comparisons presented in this thesis.

I acknowledge the University of Queensland Research Computing Centre for
access to the Bunya computing facilities used for the model evaluations.
I also thank the authors of the SIGIR 2018 study for making the anonymised
participant responses available, and the Chicago Face Database team for
providing the face images used in the experiments. These resources supported
the comparison with human judgements and the evaluation of model responses
to visual representations of gender.
