## 7. Corrections to the August thesis-plan draft that the outline already reflects

1. Polarity separation: one definition, not two; ratio ≈ 1.9×, not 2.3×.
2. Only Qwen's polarity separation is distinguishable from chance.
3. Llama-90B's ASI_intrinsic (0.153) is below what its yes-rate alone predicts (0.160).
4. δ_m depends strongly on the scale order; in the original order Qwen is +0.22.
5. The person/non-person gap survives but is smaller in the original order (Qwen 0.32).
6. The 11B HS−BS gap (seminar) does not replicate on the 90B.
7. "8 distinct values" in the invalid run refers to captured mass; p_yes had 4.
8. Two missing construct-validity threats added (gender-free referent; grid vs single image).
